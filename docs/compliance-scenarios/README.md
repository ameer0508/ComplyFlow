# Fictional Compliance Scenarios

This directory documents the fictional operational compliance use cases modeled within ComplyFlow.

---

## 📋 Scenarios Overview (Planned for Phase 2 Modeling)

1. **KYC Periodic Refresh (Know Your Customer):**
   - Scheduled periodic review of existing corporate customer documentation, source of funds, and beneficial ownership structures.
2. **PEP Screening Investigation (Politically Exposed Persons):**
   - Alert verification when an entity or associated director matches an international PEP list.
3. **AML Transaction Inquiries (Anti-Money Laundering):**
   - Secondary operational inquiries initiated by compliance after anomalous transaction thresholds are detected.
4. **Trade Sanctions Exception Review:**
   - Pre-clearance reviews for counterparties located in jurisdictions subject to partial or conditional trade restrictions.
5. **Regulatory Policy Exemption Requests:**
   - Formal requests from business units seeking temporary exemptions from internal risk thresholds or trading restrictions.

> **Disclaimer:** All scenarios, criteria, thresholds, and customer entities are 100% fictional simulations created for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.
