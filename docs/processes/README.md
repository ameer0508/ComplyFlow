# Operational Processes & SLA Framework

This directory documents the workflow diagrams, lifecycle states, SLA matrices, and escalation procedures for ComplyFlow.

---

## ⏱️ Proposed SLA Tier Matrix (Planned)

| Priority / Risk Tier | Target Turnaround (SLA) | Warning Threshold | Escalation Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Critical** | 24 Hours | 6 Hours remaining | Unresolved at 24h → Immediate Manager Escalation |
| **High** | 72 Hours (3 Days) | 24 Hours remaining | Unresolved at 72h → Team Lead Escalation |
| **Medium** | 120 Hours (5 Days) | 24 Hours remaining | Unresolved at 120h → Queue Manager Review |
| **Low** | 240 Hours (10 Days) | 48 Hours remaining | Weekly backlog report |
