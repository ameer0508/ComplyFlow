## C. Detailed Portfolio Project Version

### ComplyFlow — Compliance Workflow & Process Intelligence Platform

**Overview:**
Financial compliance teams frequently struggle with fragmented operational processes—managing urgent client reviews, sanctions exceptions, and policy sign-offs across disconnected email inboxes, spreadsheets, and chat channels. ComplyFlow is a comprehensive compliance transformation architecture designed to consolidate intake, enforce regulatory turnaround times, automate administrative handoffs, and provide executive leadership with real-time process intelligence.

**What I Built & Verified:**
1. **Synthetic Data Engine (Python):** Developed a deterministic synthetic data generator producing 150 multi-stage compliance requests, 716 immutable audit milestone events, and 4 SLA risk policies, complete with automated data validation suites verifying zero referential or date-sequence errors.
2. **Relational Database & Analytical Views (SQLite / SQL):** Implemented `complyflow.db` containing relational schemas, foreign key constraints, and 5 analytical views (`vw_request_performance`, `vw_active_backlog`, etc.) calculating cycle times and real-time backlog health against a reproducible evaluation timestamp.
3. **Power Platform Enterprise Architecture (Specifications):**
   - **Microsoft Power Apps:** Designed a 6-screen responsive Canvas application featuring role-based queue slicing, client-side input validation, and triage grids.
   - **SharePoint Online:** Architected a 3-list relational operational data layer with explicit data typing, lookup relationships, and indexing strategies.
   - **Microsoft Power Automate:** Designed 6 consolidated cloud flows managing SLA deadline calculations, automated analyst assignment alerts, multi-tier supervisory approvals, and overdue escalations.
   - **Microsoft Power BI:** Designed a dimensional star-schema model with 14 explicit DAX measures across 4 management-grade report pages (Executive Overview, Operational Workload, SLA Performance, Case Detail Triage).
4. **AI Intelligence Layer ("Ask ComplyFlow"):** Designed a read-only assistive copilot architecture providing natural-language KPI querying, 30-second case summarization, and chronological audit trail explanations, strictly grounded in governed SQL views with 11 behavioral guardrails preventing autonomous decision-making.

## E. Key Project Highlights & Talking Points

1. **Defensible Single Source of Truth:** Power BI measures and AI queries map directly to underlying SQLite analytical views rather than recalculating divergent business rules.
2. **Strict Separation of Active Backlog vs. Historical Metrics:** Cycle times and breach rates are computed strictly across completed cases (`Status = 'Closed'`), preventing open active inventory from falsely distorting regulatory performance metrics.
3. **Transparent Engineering Integrity:** Clear delineation between locally verified execution (SQLite, Python, Synthetic Data) and production implementation specifications (Power Platform, Copilot Studio), demonstrating both technical capability and professional honesty.
4. **Enterprise Governance First:** Every automated escalation and AI recommendation reinforces the non-negotiable principle: *"AI assists compliance professionals; it does not replace compliance judgment."*
